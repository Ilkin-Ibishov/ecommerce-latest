# Deep Research Playbook

Adapted from the deep-research skill. Use `#deep-research` when conducting multi-source research on market, technical, or strategic topics.

## When to Use

- Market/competitor analysis for the Azerbaijan e-commerce space
- Technology evaluation (e.g., choosing between payment providers, CDN options)
- Feature prioritization research (what do users expect?)
- Regulatory research (e-commerce laws, data protection in Azerbaijan)

## Depth Tiers

| Tier | Sources | Time | When |
|------|---------|------|------|
| Quick | 8+ | 2-5 min | Focused question, single domain |
| Standard | 15+ | 5-15 min | Market analysis, tech evaluation |
| Deep | 25+ | 15-30 min | Critical decisions, comprehensive reviews |

## Methodology

### 1. Scope
Define: research question, boundaries, depth tier, audience, output format.

### 2. Decompose
Break topic into 3-5 non-overlapping focus areas. For this project, common decompositions:
- **Market**: local competition (kontakt.az, umico.az), pricing strategy, delivery models, payment methods
- **Technical**: performance benchmarks, SEO vs competitors, infrastructure costs, scalability
- **Feature**: wishlist patterns, loyalty programs, review systems, recommendation engines

### 3. Parallel Research
Use web search with multiple angles per focus area. For Azerbaijan-specific topics, always search in both Azerbaijani and Russian — many local sources are not in English.

### 4. Triangulate
- Tier 1 (government, academic, official stats): highest weight
- Tier 2 (major publications, industry reports): high weight
- Tier 3 (blogs, company sites, promotional): supporting only
- Every major claim needs 3+ sources (2+ from Tier 1/2)
- When sources conflict: prefer primary source, present both if Tier 1 vs Tier 1

### 5. Report

Structure:
```
# [Topic]
Research Date: [date] | Depth: [tier] | Sources: [count]

## Executive Summary (200-400 words)
## Key Findings (400-1500 words each)
## Analysis (cross-cutting patterns, 500-1000 words)
## Limitations
## Recommendations (actionable next steps)
## Sources (numbered, with URL + date + tier)
```

## Quality Gates

- [ ] Source count meets tier minimum
- [ ] Every claim has inline [N] citation
- [ ] No placeholders or "TBD" entries
- [ ] Key data from last 18 months (flagged if older)
- [ ] 80%+ prose (not bullet dumps)
- [ ] Local language sources included for AZ-specific topics

## Azerbaijan Market Research Tips

- Search in Azerbaijani (`az`) and Russian (`ru`) — many local businesses have no English presence
- Check az.linkedin.com job postings for competitor hiring signals
- Local tech blogs: report.az, infocity.az, modern.az
- Government data: stat.gov.az (State Statistical Committee)
- App Store regional search: `apps.apple.com/az/` for local apps
- Competitor sites: kontakt.az, umico.az, tap.az, lalafo.az
